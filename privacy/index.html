Oyster privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · App: Oyster (com.oystervault.app)

The short version

Oyster collects nothing and sends nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server, ours or anyone else's.

What stays on your phone

The photos, videos and files you add are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored in a folder you choose on your own device. Your PIN is never stored; it is used only to unlock the encryption key on your phone. Your recovery key is shown once and never stored by the app.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Permissions

Oyster requests no Android permissions. You pick files through Android's own file picker, and when you choose to remove originals from your gallery, Android asks you to confirm.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the "Oyster Vault" folder in your file manager. Uninstalling the app does not delete that folder, so your vault survives a reinstall.

Children

Oyster is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact address shown on the app's Google Play page.
